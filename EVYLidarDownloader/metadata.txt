about=For use inside EVY: downloads through the EVY LiDAR cache (mapcache), so a tile fetched once is local for everyone. Pick an area (polygon layer or extent), choose one or more of the cached DTMs and download the tiles into a folder:
    - England: EA LIDAR Composite DTM 2022 (1 m, 2 m)
    - Wales: Welsh Government LiDAR DTM 2020-2023 (1 m)
    - Scotland: LiDAR for Scotland Phases 1-6 and the Scottish National LiDAR Programme DTM

    Other datasets, or downloads straight from the publishers, need another plugin (e.g. LIDAR Downloader UK).

    Options:
    - Build a VRT mosaic
    - Build pyramids (all levels; tiles come with the cache's ready-made overviews)
    - Merge the tiles into a single raster
    - Convert to GeoTIFF, ASCII Grid (.asc) or ESRI Float (.flt)

    Also adds Processing Toolbox algorithms (EVY LIDAR Downloader): Download LiDAR tiles, for batch runs and models, and Convert raster to FLT / ASC / GeoTIFF for any raster.
