about=Pick an area (polygon layer or extent), choose one or more LiDAR datasets and download the tiles into a folder. Sources:
    - England: Environment Agency survey catalogue (composites, time-stamped tiles, National LiDAR Programme, point clouds)
    - Scotland: Scottish Remote Sensing Portal (Phases 1-6, National LiDAR Programme, Outer Hebrides, Orkney, HES projects)
    - Wales: DataMapWales (Welsh Government LiDAR 2020-2023 and the NRW archive 1998-2015, converted from millimetres to metres)

    Options:
    - Build a VRT mosaic
    - Build pyramids (all levels)
    - Merge the tiles into a single raster
    - Convert to GeoTIFF, ASCII Grid (.asc) or ESRI Float (.flt)

    Also adds Processing Toolbox algorithms (LiDAR Fetcher UK): Download LiDAR tiles, for batch runs and models, and Convert raster to FLT / ASC / GeoTIFF for any raster.
